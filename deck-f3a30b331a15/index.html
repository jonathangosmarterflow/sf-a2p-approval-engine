Title
The Only A2P Video You Will Ever Have to Watch
The promise, kept honestly
One video: the fast way to register, why A2P gets rejected, how to fix a rejection. Three options, same finish.
No approval promise. No rate, no review time. Only two prices: $1 and $55 an hour.
Every take has ONE mode
A violet badge = the SLIDE is on camera.
A dark badge with a green dot = your SCREEN is on camera.
Each notes slide ends with NEXT: the surface the next take needs.
Screen first
Slides carry only what a screen cannot: the hook, the map, the website checklist, the legal name, the CTA.
Every screen take keeps its shown slide as the SAFETY SLIDE: 3 seconds as a title card, or stay on it if the screen take fails.
Three options. Same finish. (v7.2)
1 Ask AI, fast (takes 4, 5). 2 Chat Widget Setup, checked first (takes 8 to 11). 3 Keep your forms, Manual Setup (take 12).
Same beats, same order: what you give it, what it does, where it stops. All end on HighLevel's review screen: you approve it.
CTA: the $1 agent, or a free consulting call with a Smarter Flow consultant.
How to record
In pieces: one take at a time, each stands alone.
Every slide has a number, top right: SLIDE n (shown) or NOTE n (notes). Key H hides them.
Prompt slides (v7.3): every prompt you paste is on a shown slide after the take's card. Highlight it or tap Copy, then switch.
