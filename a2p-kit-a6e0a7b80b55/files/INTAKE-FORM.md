# A2P intake form (a2p-kit 1.3.0, sources last verified 2026-10-28)

Optional. Fill this in and paste it into your A2P Approval Agent chat, or just start chatting: the agent asks one thing at a time and skips what you already gave it. Registry compliance help, not legal advice.

> **Protect your private data before you upload anything.**
> - **Redact (black out) any SSN and the EIN digits on a document before you upload it.** The agent only needs to know that an EIN exists and the exact legal name and address as printed. You type the EIN yourself into HighLevel.
> - **Prefer typing the few fields below over uploading whole documents.**
> - **Turn off chat history / model training in your AI's settings before you start** (ChatGPT: Settings > Data Controls > "Improve the model for everyone" off, or use a Temporary Chat; Claude: Settings > Privacy, check that your chats are not used for training).

## 1. What you text (answer first; some industries can never register)
- What will your texts be about? (examples: appointment reminders, job updates, promotions, order updates)
- Do any texts mention or promote: alcohol, tobacco/vape, firearms, cannabis/CBD, prescription drugs, loans, debt relief or collection, credit repair, gambling, sweepstakes/contests/giveaways, crypto, stock tips, adult content, or leads you sell to others? (yes / no / not sure)
- Who receives them? (your customers, leads who asked for info, patients, members ...)
- How do people sign up to receive texts? (a form on your website, HighLevel chat widget, paper form, QR code, verbal, text a keyword)
- Marketing texts, non-marketing texts, or both?
- Estimated texts per day (under 2,000 / 2,000 to 6,000 / more)
