YouTube packaging data: what winners do

776 long videos from 21 creators: our HighLevel niche, AI business, general business. Titles and views were pulled on Nov 24 and 25, 2026, descriptions on Nov 25. For each creator the 10 strongest videos are compared with the 10 weakest, measured against that channel's own median views, so channel size cancels out. A gap under 10 points counts as no difference.

What it says

  1. Titles, our niche: the clearest gap is talking to the viewer. You or your is in 23% of winning titles and 3% of losing ones. Fast words: 15% against 5%.
  2. Titles, outside our niche: short loses. Under 45 characters is 19% of winners against 37% of losers in general business, and 20% against 30% in AI business. A digit helps in both (67% against 57%, 64% against 40%).
  3. A year in the title: no gap in our niche (10% against 10%), a gap in general business (17% against 7%). So the year runs as a test arm, not as the main title.
  4. Brackets at the end: general business winners use them more (26% against 10%); in our niche losers use them slightly more (56% against 46%). Not a lever for us.
  5. Descriptions: nothing separates winners from losers in any group, because each channel uses one template for every video. The description is a standard to meet, not a lever: a link in the first three lines (81% to 100% of all videos) and 1,500 to 2,400 characters.
  6. Thumbnails: three words or fewer (53 of 70 winners against 37 of 70 losers), a designed picture and not a captioned video frame (1 winner against 10 losers), words running behind the head (16 against 3).

The A2P video against this data

The Fastest Way to Get A2P Approved in GoHighLevel (My 5 Steps) (63 characters)

  • Fast word: yes. Niche winners 15%, losers 5%.
  • 63 characters: exactly the median of niche winners.
  • A digit: yes. No gap in our niche (49% against 46%), a gap among AI and general business creators.
  • No you or your, the biggest gap in our niche. That is why test title 3 says Get Your A2P Approved.
  • No year. Test title 2 carries it, so the test answers the question with your own viewers.
  • Brackets at the end: slightly more common among niche losers. All three test titles end in a bracket, so this test will not settle it.
  • Description: first link on line 2, three free links before anything else, 14 chapters, about 2,200 characters, 8 links. Standard met; nothing in the data says to change it.

3Give the face something to stand next to: one object, one drawing, or one row of things. A face and words alone is the losing layout unless the head is as tall as the frame.Face plus words and nothing else: 11 winners, 21 losers.Medium

What this data cannot say

  • Views, not click rate or watch time. Your own Studio numbers give both once the video is live, and the title test reads watch time.
  • Our niche is small: 4 of the 5 HighLevel creators watched have enough videos. A gap of 10 points there is 4 videos.
  • Niche thumbnails are not tagged yet (16 were looked at by eye). The thumbnail counts come from general business creators.
  • No search volume per phrase. YouTube's free search suggestions were used; the paid tool vidIQ would add numbers.
  • Topic moves views more than packaging does. These are habits of winners, not proof of cause.
