The top reasons A2P gets rejected

From HighLevel's own rejection codes, in five groups.

What the agent cannot open or see, it marks "Could not check" and asks you.

Name

  • Name doesn't match IRS letter (the agent checks part, you confirm the rest)
  • Gmail as the business email (the agent checks part, you confirm the rest)
  • Sole prop while you have an EIN (you check it)

A common downfall

Many owners do not know their exact legal name.

It is on your IRS letter (CP 575 or 147C). Copy it letter for letter, "LLC" or not, into the registration and your Business Profile. Type it for the agent; you do not need to upload the letter.

Website

  • Form page, not your main site (the agent checks it from your links)
  • Thin site, no text program (the agent checks it from your links)

Privacy + terms

  • Privacy policy shares your data (the agent checks it from your links)
  • Terms missing SMS rules (the agent checks it from your links)

Opt-in

  • Opt-in consent can't be seen (the agent checks it from your links)
  • No STOP, rates or frequency wording (the agent checks it from your links)
  • Other forms on the page (the agent checks it from your links)
  • Box required or bundled (the agent checks part, you confirm the rest)

Campaign

  • Description doesn't match what you send (the agent checks part, you confirm the rest)
  • Samples missing brand or STOP (you check it)
  • Business type not allowed (the agent checks part, you confirm the rest)
